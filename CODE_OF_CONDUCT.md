# Community conduct

Wirebrain welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements clearly, and help others participate. Critique ideas and code without attacking the person who shared them.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Do not post private AI conversations or account information belonging to anyone else.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or limit participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

For a public moderation concern, contact [@Saireddybv](https://github.com/Saireddybv) in the relevant issue or Discussion. For a sensitive concern, use a private contact method the maintainer lists on their GitHub profile; do not post identifying details publicly. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting channel](https://github.com/Saireddybv/wirebrain/security/advisories/new).
